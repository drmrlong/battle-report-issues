Battle Report Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Battle Report collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your army lists and matches stay on your device.

Who we are

Battle Report is an iPhone app for building army lists and tracking matches of tabletop wargames. It is made by Will Long. In this policy, "we" means Will Long.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account sign-up and does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking tools. We do not collect, sell or share any information about you.

What stays on your device

The app saves what you create in its own storage on your device:

  • your army lists
  • matches you track and your match history, including any names you type in for yourself and your opponents
  • your settings, such as text size
  • game data the app has downloaded, so it works offline

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below). It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your backup settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game data the app downloads

To show units, rules and missions, the app downloads publicly available game data from BSData on GitHub and from Wahapedia. These requests send nothing about you. Like any request on the internet, they show those servers your IP address and that the request came from the Battle Report app. Those services handle that under their own privacy policies, for example GitHub's privacy statement.

When you share

Information leaves the app only when you choose to send it: for example, sharing a match's results as an image through the iOS share sheet, or opening a link (such as our issue tracker) in your browser. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app or website you share with.

TestFlight beta

If you test Battle Report through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share some information with us, such as your name and email if you were invited by email, feedback and screenshots you submit, and crash reports. This is handled by Apple under TestFlight's privacy terms. We use it only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example by adding advertising or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/drmrlong/battle-report-issues. Issues there are public, so please don't include personal information.

Battle Report is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop.
